Our Vision

“From a student initiative to a national STEM community.”

SciNova India works to make Science, Innovation and STEM opportunities more accessible to students by connecting them with learning, mentorship, expert guidance, competitions and real-world innovation experiences.

Core Purpose · Our Mission

“To make quality Science, Innovation and STEM opportunities more accessible to students.”

Through learning, mentorship, project guidance, competitions, expert connections and community building.

02 / National Horizon

FROM
CURIOSITY
TO COMMUNITY

SciNova began as a student initiative focused on helping students explore science, develop innovative projects, improve presentations and prepare for science fairs and competitions.

Over 3–4 years, the initiative expanded through competitions, workshops, project guidance, expert sessions and student outreach.

Today, SciNova India is building a broader student-led Science, Innovation & STEM community connecting young minds with people, knowledge, and opportunities that can help them grow.
